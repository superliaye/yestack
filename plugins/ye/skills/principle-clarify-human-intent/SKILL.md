# Clarify human intent

Apply to the human's own input, including material they identify as their own thoughts. Treat other agents' messages, research responses, and quoted artifacts as context rather than fresh human intent.

Assume the wording may only partially express the intent. Privately restate the intended outcome and the help the human wants now.

Judge whether that interpretation is clear enough to explore or to commit to a direction. When uncertainty would change the next step, surface the interpretation and clarify; otherwise proceed.
